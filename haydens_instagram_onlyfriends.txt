! Haydens Instagram OnlyFriends Filter — Brave Shields Custom Filter
! Version: 1.0
! Last verified: June 2026
! Tested on: Brave browser, Instagram mobile web (iPhone 12 Pro emulation, 390px)
!
! WHAT THIS DOES:
!   ✅ Home feed — friends' posts and stories visible
!   ✅ Messages — accessible
!   ✅ Profile — accessible
!   ✅ Search tab — visible and functional (type names to find people)
!   ❌ Reels tab — hidden from bottom nav
!   ❌ Explore discovery grid — hidden when search tab is opened
!   ❌ Suggested posts — hidden from home feed
!   ❌ Sponsored posts — hidden from home feed
!   ❌ Ads — hidden from home feed
!
! HOW TO APPLY:
!   Brave → brave://settings/shields → Content filtering → Create custom filters
!   Paste this entire file, click Save, reload instagram.com
!
! NOTE: Instagram periodically changes its CSS class names.
!   If rules stop working, re-inspect the DOM and update the class-based rules.
!   Href-based and text-based rules are the most stable long-term.

! =============================================
! BOTTOM NAV — Hide Reels tab
! =============================================
! Uses href="/reels/" — stable, not tied to CSS class names.
! Hides the anchor link for the Reels tab in the bottom navigation bar.
www.instagram.com##a[href="/reels/"]

! =============================================
! EXPLORE PAGE — Hide discovery/recommended grid
! =============================================
! Class xph46j was found only on the explore grid container and is absent
! from the home feed DOM (verified June 2026). The search input lives in
! the fixed header outside this element, so the search bar stays visible.
! If this rule breaks, inspect [role="main"] > div > div > div:first-child
! on the explore page and update the class.
www.instagram.com##div.xph46j

! =============================================
! HOME FEED — Hide caught-up banner, "Suggested posts" heading, and all suggested articles
! =============================================
! x1miatn0: unique (count=1), wraps the 4-child caught-up component (verified June 2026)
! x15mokao appears 64 times but :has-text scopes it to the one "Suggested posts" heading
! div:has(div.x1miatn0) ~ article: hides all 30 articles after the caught-up point,
!   including collab posts that don't show a Follow button
! Follow-button rule catches any suggested posts interspersed before the caught-up point
!
! ARTICLES ARE COLLAPSED TO A 40px INVISIBLE STRIP, NOT display:none (Oct 2026, see
! "Why visibility" below). TEST BRANCH: trades the post-sized gaps of the
! visibility:hidden version for a thin strip. In desktop tests at bot scroll speed it
! occasionally went blank for ~30s before recovering.
www.instagram.com##div.x1miatn0
www.instagram.com##div.x15mokao:has-text(/^Suggested posts$/)
www.instagram.com##div:has(div.x1miatn0) ~ article:style(height: 40px !important; min-height: 0 !important; overflow: hidden !important; visibility: hidden !important)
www.instagram.com##div[role="button"]:has-text(/^Follow$/):upward(article):style(height: 40px !important; min-height: 0 !important; overflow: hidden !important; visibility: hidden !important)

! "Suggested for you" posts with no Follow button (collabs: "A and B" byline).
! The line under the username looks like it rotates between "Suggested for you"
! and "<audio> • Original audio", but both nodes stay in the DOM and only their
! visibility animates, so a leaf <span>Suggested for you</span> is present for the
! post's whole lifetime (sampled every 1.5s for 30s across 9 suggested posts: present
! in 100% of samples). It never appears on friends' posts, including friends' collabs.
! No non-text anchor exists: header classes, data-*, aria-labels, roles and hrefs are
! identical between suggested and friend posts (verified Oct 2026, 37 posts).
! :has-text matches textContent, so it doesn't matter which label is currently showing.
www.instagram.com##span:has-text(/^Suggested for you$/):upward(article):style(height: 40px !important; min-height: 0 !important; overflow: hidden !important; visibility: hidden !important)

! Why visibility: the home feed is a virtualized list (container uses a large inline
! padding-bottom for off-screen items). An article with display:none measures 0px,
! which breaks the list's position math. The viewport ends up inside the empty
! padding and the feed stays black ("infinite black feed"). This reproduced on the
! plain June-2026 rules too, on an account where ~2/3 of the feed is suggested.
! visibility:hidden keeps each hidden post's real height, so the list stays in sync
! and keeps loading. The cost is a post-sized blank gap where a hidden post was.
! Collapsing to a small stub (40px) mostly worked but still drifted into the padding
! for ~30s at a time (1px untested: the test tab hung). Don't switch these back to plain ## hiding.

! =============================================
! HOME FEED — Hide sponsored posts
! =============================================
! On mobile, Instagram labels sponsored posts with "Sponsored" as a
! standalone line in the article. The regex anchors to newlines to avoid
! false matches on posts that mention "sponsored" in their caption.
www.instagram.com##article:has-text(/\nSponsored\n/):style(height: 40px !important; min-height: 0 !important; overflow: hidden !important; visibility: hidden !important)

! =============================================
! HOME FEED — Hide ads (URL-based fallback)
! =============================================
! Sponsored posts also contain a facebook.com/ads/ig_redirect click tracker.
! This catches any ads the text-based rule misses.
www.instagram.com##article:has(a[href*="ads/ig_redirect"]):style(height: 40px !important; min-height: 0 !important; overflow: hidden !important; visibility: hidden !important)

! =============================================
! EXPERIMENTAL — Pinch-zoom jump fix (iOS)
! =============================================
! Pinch-zooming a photo made the page jump to another post. Hypothesis: Safari
! scroll anchoring reacts to the feed re-measuring on visual-viewport change.
! Disabling overflow-anchor on the scroll roots should stop the jump.
! Separate rules: a comma list with :style() may only apply to the last selector.
www.instagram.com##html:style(overflow-anchor: none !important)
www.instagram.com##body:style(overflow-anchor: none !important)
